Come to a massive blast door along with the decayed bodies of enclave officers, soldiers and deathclaws.

Nearby is a verticle ventilation shaft, shining a light in reveals it to be about 15ft high before it turns towards the direction of the door. 

About 5 min of crawling drops them into a waiting area in front of the security checkpoint and above them is a sign.
**CONTINUITY OF GOVERNMENT FACILITY**  
**PRESIDENTIAL RELAY SITE 01**  
**AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY**

They notice the facility is running on emergency power.


When they approach, power comes up.

A calm automated voice:

> "Restricted Continuity facility."

> "Present identification."

They obviously don't have any.

> "Identification not recognized."

Pause.

> "Please remain stationary."

That sounds almost polite.

Then they hear machinery moving behind the walls.

Pause for 15 seconds

Sentry Bots come from wall panels and turrets drop from the ceiling.

> "Unauthorized personnel confirmed."

**Then the defenses activate.**





Beyond that checkpoint they discover evidence of the post-war Enclave expedition.

The expedition established a small staging area just inside the portion of the complex they managed to penetrate.

There are portable Enclave work lights, bedrolls, ration containers, ammunition, a portable generator, cutting equipment, diagnostic equipment, several weapons, and a **"field terminal"** wired into the facility.

There are three bodies here: one killed by facility security, one badly mauled by a Deathclaw during the expedition's retreat, and one killed by catastrophic injuries from attempting a physical breach.

### Their field terminal is the important discovery

>

> 
> Inner facility inaccessible.

### Their expedition map

This is probably the most practically useful thing they left behind.

The Enclave mapped what they could reach and marked several locations:

> **SECURITY CONTROL**  
> **FACILITY ADMINISTRATION**  
> **POWER DISTRIBUTION**  
> **RELAY CONTROL — INACCESSIBLE**  
> **UNKNOWN SERVICE ACCESS**

There are handwritten notes such as:

> "Turrets here."

> "Door mechanically interlocked."

> "Possible bypass?"

> "NO ACCESS — authorization system offline."

This gives your players information without telling them what route to take.

### Their technical notes

The engineer figured out something important before the expedition failed:

The security system isn't a single system.

There are separate layers:

**Physical security** — blast doors, locks, shutters.

**Personnel security** — badges, biometrics, clearance.

**Continuity security** — authorization required for sensitive systems like Relay Control.

So the players immediately understand that simply stealing an Enclave officer's badge isn't going to solve everything.

More importantly, the engineer identified **Facility Administration** as the likely location of the personnel/security database.

That's their strongest lead.

### A recovered Enclave credential

I would give them one.

Not because it opens everything, but because it creates options.

The expedition commander's credential is recognized as legitimate federal/Enclave authority. That can get them through certain general government security systems and potentially stop some automated defenses from immediately treating them as random intruders.

But when used against Continuity systems:

> **FEDERAL AUTHORITY: VERIFIED**  
> **CONTINUITY CLEARANCE: NOT FOUND**

That's a useful distinction.

### The last recording

I'd keep this short.

The expedition leader realizes Old Olney is becoming untenable and orders withdrawal:

> "We've lost two people getting this far."
> 
> "Engineering says we're not getting through the inner doors without bringing the site online."
> 
> "And apparently every Deathclaw in Maryland has decided to investigate the generator."
> 
> "We're pulling out."
> 
> "Leave the equipment."
> 
> "If Command ever restores the Continuity network, they can send another team."

Then something goes wrong during the withdrawal.

That explains the abandoned camp and bodies without making the Enclave incompetent.

And there's a nice realization for the players:

**They are that "next team."**

Not because anyone sent them, but because the prerequisite the Enclave expedition was waiting for has finally happened.

From here, the expedition's map naturally points them toward **Facility Administration**, which I think should be their next major area—and where we can build the actual credential/security problem they need to solve to reach Relay Control.

### Facility Administration

The Enclave map leads them through a security door marked:

> **FACILITY ADMINISTRATION**  
> **PERSONNEL • SECURITY • OPERATIONS**

Unlike the outer facility, this area looks like offices: reception/security desk, records, Site Director's office, communications office, conference room, and personnel/security control.

Because the tower reactivated the facility, lights and terminals are now functioning for the first time since the Enclave expedition.

I'd put four useful discoveries here.

**Personnel Records:** They can access the employee roster. Most personnel evacuated or abandoned their posts during the Great War, but one entry stands out:

> **SITE DIRECTOR — [Name]**  
> Clearance: CONTINUITY-5  
> Status: **ON SITE**

This tells them somebody with very high authorization was still here when the bombs fell.

**Security Office:** This gives them control over portions of the facility. They can see cameras, door states and automated defenses. However, Relay Control is isolated from ordinary security administration.

They can potentially disable turrets, unlock secondary doors, or locate alternate routes—but not simply open Relay Control.

**Site Director's Office:** This should contain the most interesting lore. The office has been sealed since 2077. Inside are personal correspondence, Continuity orders and a terminal showing the escalating situation on October 23.

The final entry could be:

> **10/23/77 — 09:47**
> 
> National emergency protocols received.
> 
> Relay Site 01 placed under Continuity authority.
> 
> Presidential Relay synchronization pending.
> 
> All nonessential personnel released.
> 
> Remaining staff ordered to Shelter Level.

Then:

> **10/23/77 — 09:52**
> 
> Pentagon link lost.
> 
> Raven Rock link lost.
> 
> Network synchronization incomplete.
> 
> Proceeding to Emergency Operations.

So the Site Director didn't die sitting conveniently behind his desk.

He went somewhere deeper.

### The important discovery

Administration reveals that access to Relay Control requires **two things**:

> **CONTINUITY-5 PERSONNEL AUTHORIZATION**  
> **SECURITY CONTROL AUTHENTICATION**

That gives you a nice two-part problem instead of "find keycard."

Administration can provide the second component.

The players can hack Security Control, use the Enclave bypass equipment, repair the authentication server, manipulate the personnel database, etc.

But they still need someone with Continuity-5 clearance.

The only person they know had it was the Site Director.

And according to the last personnel tracking record:

> **DIRECTOR [NAME]**  
> LAST BADGE EVENT:  
> **SHELTER LEVEL — EMERGENCY OPERATIONS**

Now they have their next objective.




Before reaching the relay chamber, they encounter a central security control room.

This is where I'd give them their biggest choice.

From here they can potentially disable the remaining automated defenses.

But doing so requires either a difficult Science/Repair test or physically shutting down the security power bus.

If they simply cut power, they discover:

> **WARNING: SECURITY BUS SHARES AUXILIARY POWER WITH RELAY CONTROL**

They can still do it.

It makes the rest of the facility substantially safer, but they'll later need to restore/reroute power before they can use Relay Site 01.

Alternatively, they can leave everything running and continue dealing with security.

### 5. One serious defensive encounter

I'd put **one genuinely dangerous pre-war defense** immediately before the inner facility.

Something beyond ordinary Protectrons.

My preference would be an **Enclave prototype security robot** specifically built for Continuity installations—something the PCs haven't seen before.

It doesn't need to be a mandatory boss.

They could fight it, spoof its authorization, shut it down from Security, trap it behind a blast door, sneak around it through maintenance access, etc.

If they fight it, make it nasty enough that they understand why the Enclave expedition eventually decided this facility wasn't worth additional casualties.

### 6. The door the Enclave couldn't open

Finally they reach the actual Relay Site 01 blast door.

Evidence shows the Enclave tried everything.

Explosives.

Cutting equipment.

Terminal intrusion.

Presidential credentials.

Nothing worked.

Their last log explains why:

> **LOCAL AUTHORITY: VERIFIED**  
> **EXECUTIVE AUTHORITY: VERIFIED**  
> **CONTINUITY NETWORK: OFFLINE**  
> **ACCESS DENIED**

This wasn't really a lock they could crack. **The relay required authentication from another active Continuity node.**

Now the PCs approach.

The system begins checking authorization.

They may actually initially receive:

> **ACCESS DENIED**

Then the system detects something that wasn't present when the Enclave came through:

> **CONTINUITY RELAY NETWORK: ONLINE**  
> **REMOTE NODE DETECTED**  
> **SYNCHRONIZING...**

That's the radio tower coming back into play.

The players don't automatically get access just because they're special. **A previous action changed the state of the world**, making something possible now that wasn't possible for the Enclave.

Then the massive door opens.

I think those six beats are enough. I wouldn't make the facility much longer than that because you still want time in the session for **Relay Site 01 → WMATA → BLACKLINE → Nahk camera reveal**.
